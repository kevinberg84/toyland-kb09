01 / DISCOVER KB-09

A personal synth, ready to explore

KB-09 brings Kevin's Roland SH-09 into a playable software instrument, with a hands-on panel modeled after his own aged synth. One oscillator, a sub oscillator, a resonant low-pass filter and a flexible envelope give you a direct path from a deep bass to a singing lead, a hollow pulse or a burst of noise percussion. Reach for a fader, switch a waveform and hear the sound take shape. Sixteen factory sounds offer starting points, and you can save every adjustment as your own preset.

Modeled after the real SH-09 circuitry. The original Roland service schematic guides KB-09's signal path, with particular attention to the filter: four nonlinear OTA stages, based on the SH-09's BA662 stages, interact with the resonance feedback and its opposing limiting diodes. That feedback produces the filter's self-oscillating tone and analog-style harmonic character. Explore the filter model.

AGE gives the instrument a condition of its own: the panel wears, the keys yellow and the circuit model's pitch calibration, filter response and envelope timing shift subtly. You can also bring in drums, guitar, vocals or another synth through External Input, shape them with the filter, and use the envelope follower to open it with the incoming signal's level: a touch-sensitive auto-wah that digs in harder when you play harder. Play KB-09 in your DAW or open the standalone app for a session with just a MIDI keyboard.

03 / FIRST SOUND

Start playing in a minute

  1. Open the standalone app, or install the version for your computer and load Toyland KB-09 on a DAW instrument/MIDI track.
  2. Choose 01 / Calibrated Init in the preset field along the top.
  3. Turn down your listening level, play a MIDI keyboard (enable MIDI monitoring if using a DAW), or enable KEYBOARD in the standalone app. You can also click KB-09's keys.
  4. Move CUTOFF FREQ to hear the sound darken or brighten. Add a little RESONANCE for a stronger filter peak.
  5. Try 02 / Fundamental Bass or 04 / Singing Lead, then save a sound of your own.

KB-09 is monophonic: it plays one note at a time, with the lowest held note taking priority. Overlapping notes and the envelope trigger switch shape its legato behaviour. Click and hold an onscreen key, or hold a MIDI note, to see the key depress and darken. A small amber bar along the key's lower edge identifies the active note; other held keys remain shaded without a coloured marker. The marks disappear when the keys are released.

No DAW? Use the standalone app. Toyland KB-09 Standalone is a complete playable virtual synth. Choose your audio output and MIDI input in its audio/MIDI settings, then select a preset and play. It does not record a song or replace a DAW.

04 / CHARACTER

Meet AGE

Choose the condition of your instrument.

CONDITION / AGE lives below the instrument. At 0 it selects the healthy calibration profile. Turn it up to explore a more played-in instrument, then turn it back whenever you like. AGE is saved with your sound and works in both Authentic and Modern modes.

Click anywhere on the AGE slider track to jump straight to that condition, or drag its cap; hold SHIFT for fine dragging. The readout uses these condition names while the underlying age remains continuous:

The ten AGE nicknames

Nice 'n' New → Barely Broken In → Like New-ish → Lightly Loved → Seen Some Shit → Vintage, Baby! → Sold As-Is → Smells Like 1983 → Fire Hazard → Who Let You Have This?

Each name covers the next tenth of the slider; the last includes its maximum. The condition changes continuously between them. While dragging, AGE uses blended previews so the cap responds immediately and the appearance changes without releasing the mouse; full detail returns when you let go.

Inspired by Kevin's own aged SH-09, AGE changes both the synth's appearance and its circuit behavior.

What changes?

Appearance

Yellowing, dust, dulling and tarnish build gradually. Stains, small scuffs, uneven key gaps and fine cracks add to the wear. Three fader caps disappear progressively from SOLD AS-IS onward and the bender pointer breaks off at FIRE HAZARD, revealing the parts beneath.

The controls continue to work through every condition. Lower AGE whenever you want to bring back the caps, intact bender and fresher panel.

Sound

AGE gradually changes calibration, envelope timing and the filter's nonlinear response. Fresh settings already have analog-style colour; worn settings add a little asymmetry and reduce headroom. The same setting gives the same condition each time, so it can be saved with a patch.

AGE is a creative condition control rather than a clock counting elapsed years. It does not add random crackles or make a worn key stop playing.

Try this

Save a favourite patch. Hold one note and move AGE slowly from fresh to worn. Compare the subtle sound changes, then explore the filter and envelope with AGE at a setting you like. Inspect the panel at L size, and load your saved patch whenever you want to return to the starting point.

05 / HANDS ON

Learn the panel

Drag sliders vertically; drag a knob vertically or horizontally. Hover or drag to read its value bubble. Most sound controls use a 0–10 display; TUNE displays cents. Double-click a continuous control to return to its default. Click or drag the ridged switches, or click their adjacent labels. Focused switches also accept arrow keys, Home and End.

Click the grey POWER button to switch the instrument on or off. It stands tall when OFF and sits lower when ON; the red lamp below the POWER label lights when the synth is on. Switching it off mutes the sound and clears held notes. Play a fresh note after switching it back on.

Play the filter itself

Lower VCO, SUB OSC and EXT to zero, set VCA to HOLD, lower VCF MOD and ENV, then raise RESONANCE until the filter sings. CUTOFF FREQ sets its pitch; the keyboard and VCF bender depth also move it. Start with a low listening level.

Modeled after the SH-09 filter circuitry

KB-09's filter is modeled after the real Roland SH-09 circuitry, using the original service schematic as its reference. Particular attention goes to the four BA662 OTA stages and the opposing resonance-limiting diodes, represented as interacting nonlinear stages and a separate diode feedback model. Their feedback is solved together at each internal sample. Self-oscillation grows in that feedback loop; it is not a separate sine generator or an added distortion/noise effect. Processing at eight times the host sample rate reduces artifacts from the nonlinear response.

This is a circuit-informed, reduced model. Its voltage scale and component tolerances have not been calibrated against Kevin's hardware, and AGE represents a chosen condition rather than measured decades of wear. See the original Roland service notes for the circuit behind it.

The revised resonance response in 1.1.4 is stronger and changes self-oscillation pitch somewhat at the same CUTOFF setting. Saved patches and automation still load normally; high-resonance sounds may need a small cutoff or level adjustment.

Authentic & Modern

Authentic keeps the original-style routing and ignores the optional velocity, aftertouch, LFO tempo-sync and input-trim settings. Those controls dim. Modern enables them: velocity controls level, channel aftertouch opens the filter, SYNC locks RATE to musical grid divisions, and INPUT TRIM adjusts external input by ±24 dB. Return to Modern to use their retained settings.

Modern provides deeper maximum LFO modulation: ±36 semitones for VCO pitch and ±8 octaves for the VCF, compared with Authentic's ±12 semitones and ±5 octaves. These are software design ranges. Modern sounds saved before 0.11 with nonzero MOD can sound deeper after upgrading. The bender sensitivities are separate.

The header's ENV and EXT indicators show envelope/auto-wah follower activity. EXT AUDIO opens a routing reminder in the same dialog style as the other KB-09 windows; choose OK to close.

06 / YOUR SOUNDS

Save your sounds

  1. Adjust the panel, including AGE and the Authentic/Modern choice.
  2. Click SAVE, choose a location you can find again and give the sound a name. KB-09 writes a .kb09 file.
  3. To recall it, click LOAD and select that .kb09 file.

SAVE starts in Documents / Toyland KB-09 Presets, with writable copies of all sixteen factory patches. Choose another folder whenever you like. LOAD starts in the folder where you most recently saved a patch, even after reopening KB-09. Loading a patch from elsewhere does not change that default.

Closing the standalone: choose Save, Don't Save, or Cancel. Use the arrow keys to switch the highlighted Save / Don't Save / Cancel choice, Enter to choose it, or Esc to cancel. Save lets you name a .kb09 file and opens that saved patch next launch. Don't Save starts the next launch with Calibrated Init. Cancel leaves the app open; cancelling the save dialog or a failed save also leaves it open. If the saved startup file is moved or deleted, KB-09 falls back to Calibrated Init. DAW projects continue to restore their own plug-in state.

The loaded filename appears in the preset field. The sixteen factory presets remain available; selecting one recalls its original settings. Save your edits before choosing another sound. Cancelling a file dialog or rejecting an invalid preset leaves the current sound unchanged.

A preset saves the sound parameters, including AGE. It does not save held notes, the current envelope position, power or window placement. Your DAW also saves the patch with its project; external track routing belongs to that DAW project.

09 / BEYOND THE OSCILLATOR

External audio: your touch-sensitive auto-wah

KB-09's VST3 instrument exposes an External Input auxiliary audio bus. A DAW may call this a sidechain. Here the incoming audio can become part of the sound, passing through the mixer, filter and amplifier.

  1. Route an audio source to the instrument's External Input.
  2. Choose 12 / External Filter. It lowers the oscillators, raises EXT and uses VCA Hold, so no MIDI note is needed.
  3. Adjust CUTOFF FREQ and RESONANCE.
  4. Try 13 / External Follower: louder input opens the filter for a touch-sensitive auto-wah. Adjust the VCF ENV amount and the source level.

For auto-wah, the follower listens before the EXT mixer slider. Lowering EXT can make the source quieter while its filter modulation remains active. The follower does not trigger ADSR or open the VCA; use Hold for continuous processing. Stereo input is averaged to mono, so opposite-polarity channels can cancel. Output is mono duplicated to stereo.

Roland already described touch-sensitive wah effects in the original SH-09 owner's manual (external-instrument examples). KB-09 brings that expressive idea into your software setup. Try short guitar chords or a drum loop, lower CUTOFF, add RESONANCE and raise ENV until each attack opens the filter.

In Ableton Live

  1. Place KB-09 on a MIDI track; put the source audio on another track.
  2. Expand the plug-in device's Sidechain panel. Enable Sidechain, choose the source under Audio From and select the intended tap, such as Post FX.
  3. Set the sidechain mix to 100% for a known starting point, then choose External Filter in KB-09.
  4. Monitor the processed path clearly. Prevent the source's direct output from masking the filtered result.
  5. Save the Live Set to retain the track routing, then reopen it and verify the source and patch.

See the Live 11 sidechain routing guide. Menu names may vary in later versions. In another DAW, use its auxiliary-input or sidechain routing to send audio into KB-09's External Input.

Sounds, settings and ideas to return to:

Pitch-bender readout

Hover over or drag the spring-return bender to see its signed position, from -100% to +100%; centre is 0%. The VCO and VCF depth faders determine the resulting pitch and filter movement, so the position is not a fixed semitone amount. Release the bender to return to centre.

A little more control, a lot more playing

Hold SHIFT while dragging any knob, fader or the bender for ten-times finer adjustment. Release SHIFT to return to normal speed, even during a drag. AGE changes the sound, slider position and appearance during the drag. Blended visual previews keep it responsive; full visual detail returns on release. The four utility sliders have larger handles and grab areas. Faders move relative to where you grab them, so a fine adjustment will not jump to the pointer. The DESCRIPTION button in the bottom strip enables hover descriptions; it starts off and lights amber with DESCRIPTION ON when enabled. Hover over DESCRIPTION itself for help even when it is off. Value readouts work with DESCRIPTION off or on. Switches show their selected mode in a readable value box when hovered or adjusted; the box disappears after you leave or finish the adjustment.

VOLUME now has an audio taper: quiet levels occupy useful knob travel, 0 mutes, and 10 is full output. Existing patches keep their knob positions, so intermediate VOLUME settings sound quieter than in 0.13.2; turn up VOLUME if needed.

In Modern, choose FREE for the continuous original 0.2–25 Hz LFO, or SYNC to follow the host tempo. RATE then selects 21 divisions, from four bars to 1/64-note triplets. Its value box displays the division. D means dotted (one-and-a-half times the straight duration); T means triplet (two-thirds). Bar labels assume 4/4; divisions are quarter-note beat durations and do not change with the host time signature. Without host tempo information the clock uses 120 BPM. Authentic always uses the original free rate, even with a saved SYNC setting.

The RATE lamp follows the LFO clock with clear half-cycle flashes below 8 Hz. At 8 Hz and above it becomes a steady glow that brightens with rate: this avoids misleading flicker when a screen cannot show each cycle. Modulation and LFO envelope triggering continue at the actual selected rate. SYNC follows tempo, not the host transport's bar position; stopping playback does not reset its phase.

The header's IN meter shows audio reaching the plug-in before the AUDIO MIXER's EXT fader and before stereo is folded to mono. ENV shows the synth envelope; EXT shows the external auto-wah envelope follower. MIDI flashes when notes, pitch bend or other MIDI channel messages arrive. Enable DESCRIPTION and hover over an indicator for an explanation. IN activity with silence at the output means you should check EXT level, VCA mode and filter cutoff. Opposite-polarity stereo channels can light IN yet cancel in the mono signal.

On Windows, drag KB-09's own top header to the top of the screen and release to enter L. Pull the header down to restore the size you used before L. Compatible floating plug-in windows also support their native title bar; a host that controls placement may require the plug-in's own header or the S/M/L buttons. L fills the available screen work area while preserving the panel's proportions.

The instructions offer Open download folder and Copy instructions; both leave the instructions open. Copy them before closing KB-09. No update downloads or installs itself.

Your saved presets and audio/MIDI preferences are kept. Save your current patch before closing KB-09. The normal preset folder is Documents/Toyland KB-09 Presets. Keep personal presets stored inside an old extracted download folder before replacing it.

  1. Download the Windows or Mac ZIP and extract the entire ZIP.
  2. Save your patch and close KB-09. If using plug-ins, also close your DAW.
  3. For a portable standalone, replace the old extracted folder, keeping personal presets, and open the new standalone copy. For an installed copy, run INSTALL KB-09.cmd (Windows) or INSTALL KB-09.command (Mac). Both offer an optional desktop shortcut. On Mac the standalone installs in your home Applications folder; AU/VST3 installation is optional and requires your Mac login password.
  4. Use the new copy or installed shortcut thereafter. Confirm the new version under TOYLAND / KB-09. Reopen your DAW and rescan plug-ins if necessary.

Update-check preferences belong to your Windows or Mac account, apply across projects, and are not stored in presets. Failed automatic checks stay quiet. A failed manual check shows a retry message.

11 / QUICK FIXES

Solve a problem

In the standalone app, Ctrl+W (Windows) or Cmd+W (Mac) closes the window through the same Save / Don’t Save / Cancel prompt as the close button.

What do the version numbers mean?

KB-09 uses major.minor.revision numbers. Version 1.0.0 is the first released version. The numbers are major.minor.patch: fixes advance the patch number (1.0.1), new features advance the minor number (1.2.0), and major changes advance the major number (2.0.0). Each release gets a distinct version for bug reports and update checks. These numbers are labels, not decimal fractions or dates.

I cannot find KB-09 in my DAW

Extract the download, run INSTALL KB-09.cmd, and rescan VST3 plug-ins in your music software. Look under instruments. Use a 64-bit VST3 host. Logic requires AU; use the separate Mac download for AU.

I hear nothing

Load Calibrated Init, switch POWER on and play a fresh note. Check track monitoring, MIDI input, VOLUME and the DAW output. Choose OK to close the EXT AUDIO dialog before playing. External presets need an audio source. In standalone, check audio/MIDI settings.

A sound keeps playing

VCA HOLD deliberately passes audio without a key. Try ENV or GATE, or load Calibrated Init. POWER mutes and clears note history; play a new note after turning it back on.

The window is large but blurry

Use the DAW-specific scaling setting. In Live, disabling Auto-Scale Plug-in Window fixed this for the owner. Then choose KB-09's own size. Do not lower the screen resolution to make the plug-in bigger.

The footer or a control is outside the window

Choose S or M, reopen the editor and check the host's scaling setting. Try a fresh instance after saving the patch. If it persists, note the DAW version, Windows display scale, monitor resolution and selected size when reporting the problem; a host can constrain a plug-in's requested dimensions.

Velocity, aftertouch, sync or input trim does nothing

Select Modern. Authentic deliberately ignores these optional controls. Aftertouch also needs a controller that sends channel pressure. Onscreen mouse presses do not provide expressive aftertouch.

External audio is quiet or disappears

Confirm the host enabled the auxiliary input, raise EXT and use VCA Hold. Stereo sources with opposite-polarity channels cancel when folded to mono. In Modern, check INPUT TRIM. IN shows the incoming audio peak before mono folding and mixer level; EXT shows the auto-wah envelope follower. These meters do not promise that your output path is audible.

Windows reports a missing VCRUNTIME or MSVCP file

Install Microsoft's current x64 Visual C++ Redistributable from Microsoft's official download page, then reopen the synth. Do not download individual DLLs from unrelated sites.

A missing cap, cracked key or broken bender looks alarming

These are visual AGE effects. The controls continue to work. Lower AGE to restore a fresher appearance; the same setting also returns the sound toward its healthy profile.

Made for curiosity & playing

KB-09 is an unofficial personal recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09, using a circuit-informed digital model and artwork modeled after his own aged instrument. “Authentic” names the original-style routing mode; individual hardware instruments can differ. The instrument has one voice, no onboard effects, no sequencer and no MIDI Learn. It reports 128 samples of processing latency. Hardware Gate/CV sockets, alternate tunings and per-note expressive protocols are not provided. Use your DAW for recording, effects and arrangement.
