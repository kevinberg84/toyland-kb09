Toyland KB-09

A recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09.

AGE lets you age both the instrument's appearance and its circuit-model behavior, from a fresh panel to yellowed keys, dust and worn metal. Shape a synth sound by hand, or send external audio through the filter and let the envelope follower move it with your playing.

01 / DISCOVER KB-09

A personal synth, ready to explore

KB-09 brings Kevin's Roland SH-09 into a playable software instrument, with a hands-on panel modeled after his own aged synth. One oscillator, a sub oscillator, a resonant low-pass filter and a flexible envelope give you a direct path from a deep bass to a singing lead, a hollow pulse or a burst of noise percussion. Reach for a fader, switch a waveform and hear the sound take shape. Sixteen factory sounds offer starting points, and you can save every adjustment as your own preset.

AGE gives that instrument a condition of its own: the panel wears, the keys yellow and the circuit model's pitch calibration, filter response and envelope timing shift subtly. You can also bring in drums, guitar, vocals or another synth through External Input, shape them with the filter, and use the envelope follower to open it with the incoming signal's level. Play KB-09 in your DAW or open the standalone app for a session with just a MIDI keyboard.

02 / SETUP

Install & open

Windows VST3

  1. Extract the supplied download folder first. Locate the entire Toyland KB-09.vst3 bundle. On Windows this may look like a folder.
  2. Copy that bundle, including its Contents folder, to:
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
    Windows may ask for administrator permission when copying into Program Files.
  3. Open your DAW and rescan VST3 plug-ins if KB-09 does not appear. Select it as an instrument, then enable MIDI monitoring.

Use a 64-bit Windows DAW with VST3 support. Do not copy only the inner module, rename the bundle or place two versions of KB-09 in scanned locations. The earlier SH-09 development name shares its identity; keep any backup outside DAW plug-in folders.

Standalone

Keep Toyland KB-09.exe in a convenient folder and open it directly. Its panel and factory sounds are embedded; no separate graphics installation is needed. Select the correct audio device and MIDI keyboard in the standalone settings. If another app is using an exclusive audio driver, close that app or choose a shared device.

03 / FIRST SOUND

Start playing in a minute

  1. Install the Windows VST3, then load Toyland KB-09 on an instrument or MIDI track.
  2. Choose 01 / Calibrated Init in the preset field along the top.
  3. Turn down your listening level, enable the track's MIDI monitoring and play a MIDI keyboard. You can also click KB-09's keys.
  4. Move CUTOFF FREQ to hear the sound darken or brighten. Add a little RESONANCE for a stronger filter peak.
  5. Try 02 / Fundamental Bass or 04 / Singing Lead, then save a sound of your own.

KB-09 is monophonic: it plays one note at a time, with the lowest held note taking priority. Overlapping notes and the envelope trigger switch shape its legato behaviour. Click and hold an onscreen key, or hold a MIDI note, to see its blue highlight. A small amber bar along the key's lower edge identifies the note currently playing; other held keys keep a blue bar. The marks disappear when the keys are released.

No DAW? Use the standalone app. Toyland KB-09.exe is a complete playable virtual synth. Choose your audio output and MIDI input in its audio/MIDI settings, then select a preset and play. It does not record a song or replace a DAW.

04 / CHARACTER

Meet AGE

Choose the condition of your instrument.

CONDITION / AGE lives below the instrument. At 0 it selects the healthy calibration profile. Turn it up to explore a more played-in instrument, then turn it back whenever you like. AGE is saved with your sound and works in both Authentic and Modern modes.

The wear is modeled after Kevin's own aged SH-09. Dust settles in slider openings, around knob skirts and in grip ribs; keys warm to ivory and lose their shine; printing discolours; and worn panel paint reveals the metal beneath. The knobs' metal surfaces dull and develop fine pitting, while pale oxidation gathers on the screws. Explore the look at a larger size to see the small details.

What changes?

Appearance

Yellowing, dust, dulling and tarnish build gradually. Distinct stains appear separately and keep their shape once present, including a coffee ring in the envelope section. One key develops a fine hairline crack that can extend a little. Later wear can remove a fader cap or two and break the bender tip, revealing the parts beneath.

The controls continue to work through every condition. Lower AGE whenever you want to bring back the caps, intact bender and fresher panel.

Sound

AGE subtly changes pitch calibration, filter tuning/resonance and envelope timing within the circuit model. The same setting gives the same condition each time, so you can save it with a patch and return to it.

AGE is a creative condition control rather than a clock counting elapsed years. It does not add random crackles or make a worn key stop playing.

Try this

Save a favourite patch. Hold one note and move AGE slowly from fresh to worn. Compare the subtle sound changes, then explore the filter and envelope with AGE at a setting you like. Inspect the panel at L size, and load your saved patch whenever you want to return to the starting point.

05 / HANDS ON

Learn the panel

Drag sliders vertically; drag a knob vertically or horizontally. Hover or drag to read its value bubble. Most sound controls use a 0–10 display; TUNE displays cents. Double-click a continuous control to return to its default. Click or drag the ridged switches, or click their adjacent labels. Focused switches also accept arrow keys, Home and End.

Click the grey POWER button to switch the instrument on or off. It stands tall when OFF and sits lower when ON; the red lamp below the POWER label lights when the synth is on. Switching it off mutes the sound and clears held notes. Play a fresh note after switching it back on.

Authentic & Modern

Authentic keeps the original-style routing and ignores the optional velocity, aftertouch, LFO tempo-sync and input-trim settings. Those controls dim. Modern enables them: velocity controls level, channel aftertouch opens the filter, TEMPO syncs the LFO, and INPUT TRIM adjusts external input by ±24 dB. Return to Modern to use their retained settings.

Modern provides deeper maximum LFO modulation: ±36 semitones for VCO pitch and ±8 octaves for the VCF, compared with Authentic's ±12 semitones and ±5 octaves. These are software design ranges. Modern sounds saved before 0.11 with nonzero MOD can sound deeper after upgrading. The bender sensitivities are separate.

The header's ENV and EXT indicators show envelope/follower activity. EXT AUDIO opens a routing reminder over the keyboard; click it again to close.

06 / YOUR SOUNDS

Save your sounds

  1. Adjust the panel, including AGE and the Authentic/Modern choice.
  2. Click SAVE, choose a location you can find again and give the sound a name. KB-09 writes a .kb09 file.
  3. To recall it, click LOAD and select that file. Compatible earlier .sh09state files also load.

The loaded filename appears in the preset field. The sixteen factory presets remain available; selecting one recalls its original settings. Save your edits before choosing another sound. Cancelling a file dialog or rejecting an invalid preset leaves the current sound unchanged.

A preset saves the sound parameters, including AGE. It does not save held notes, the current envelope position, power or window placement. Your DAW also saves the patch with its project; external track routing belongs to that DAW project.

For a blurry KB-09 window on Windows, find the plug-in in Live's Browser, right-click it and disable Auto-Scale Plug-in Window, if offered. Kevin confirmed on 7 October 2026 that this fixed the display in his Live setup.

Then use KB-09's S, M or L button for size. If the change is not reflected, close/reopen the editor. If needed, save the patch and try a freshly inserted instance.

Cubase / Nuendo

Keep the DAW's global Enable HiDPI option on. Open the plug-in window's Functions menu, or its unused-space context menu. If present, leave Allow Window to be Resized unchecked so KB-09 can handle its own scale. Steinberg provides that option to help plug-ins without their own DPI handling.

Close/reopen the editor and try a fresh instance if needed. Use KB-09's own S/M/L buttons to choose a comfortable size.

Studio One / Fender Studio Pro

Retain the application's High-DPI mode. In the plug-in editor tab's settings menu, turn Enable System DPI Scaling off when offered. That option makes older small interfaces larger through bitmap scaling and can blur a plug-in that already handles its own scale.

Reopen the editor, and save your patch before trying a new instance if needed. For Studio One 7, search the setting name in Help > Studio One Reference Manual; the current online manual is branded Fender Studio Pro 8.1, so its application menu name differs.

FL Studio

Open the plug-in wrapper's additional settings and its Settings tab. Enable DPI aware so the plug-in handles its own scale. Keep KB-09's own size buttons as the normal sizing controls.

Logic Pro / GarageBand / Pro Tools Release unavailable

09 / BEYOND THE OSCILLATOR

Filter external audio

KB-09's VST3 instrument exposes an External Input auxiliary audio bus. A DAW may call this a sidechain. Here the incoming audio can become part of the sound, passing through the mixer, filter and amplifier.

  1. Route an audio source to the instrument's External Input.
  2. Choose 12 / External Filter. It lowers the oscillators, raises EXT and uses VCA Hold, so no MIDI note is needed.
  3. Adjust CUTOFF FREQ and RESONANCE.
  4. Try 13 / External Follower: louder input opens the filter. Adjust the VCF ENV amount and the source level.

The follower listens before the EXT mixer slider. Lowering EXT can make the source quieter while its filter modulation remains active. The follower does not trigger ADSR or open the VCA; use Hold for continuous processing. Stereo input is averaged to mono, so opposite-polarity channels can cancel. Output is mono duplicated to stereo.

In Ableton Live

  1. Place KB-09 on a MIDI track; put the source audio on another track.
  2. Expand the plug-in device's Sidechain panel. Enable Sidechain, choose the source under Audio From and select the intended tap, such as Post FX.
  3. Set the sidechain mix to 100% for a known starting point, then choose External Filter in KB-09.
  4. Monitor the processed path clearly. Prevent the source's direct output from masking the filtered result.
  5. Save the Live Set to retain the track routing, then reopen it and verify the source and patch.

See the Live 11 sidechain routing guide. Menu names may vary in later versions. In another DAW, use its auxiliary-input or sidechain routing to send audio into KB-09's External Input.

10 / QUICK FIXES

Solve a problem

I cannot find KB-09 in my DAW

Confirm you installed the entire .vst3 bundle in the VST3 folder, extracted the download, and rescanned. Look under instruments. Use a 64-bit VST3 host. Logic requires a built AU, and ordinary Pro Tools requires a signed AAX release; those versions are unavailable in this package.

I hear nothing

Load Calibrated Init, switch POWER on and play a fresh note. Check track monitoring, MIDI input, VOLUME and the DAW output. Close the EXT AUDIO overlay if it covers the keyboard. External presets need an audio source. In standalone, check audio/MIDI settings.

A sound keeps playing

VCA HOLD deliberately passes audio without a key. Try ENV or GATE, or load Calibrated Init. POWER mutes and clears note history; play a new note after turning it back on.

The window is large but blurry

Use the DAW-specific scaling setting. In Live, disabling Auto-Scale Plug-in Window fixed this for the owner. Then choose KB-09's own size. Do not lower the screen resolution to make the plug-in bigger.

The footer or a control is outside the window

Choose S or M, reopen the editor and check the host's scaling setting. Try a fresh instance after saving the patch. If it persists, note the DAW version, Windows display scale, monitor resolution and selected size when reporting the problem; a host can constrain a plug-in's requested dimensions.

Velocity, aftertouch, tempo or input trim does nothing

Select Modern. Authentic deliberately ignores these optional controls. Aftertouch also needs a controller that sends channel pressure. Onscreen mouse presses do not provide expressive aftertouch.

External audio is quiet or disappears

Confirm the host enabled the auxiliary input, raise EXT and use VCA Hold. Stereo sources with opposite-polarity channels cancel when folded to mono. In Modern, check INPUT TRIM. The EXT meter tracks activity; it is not a promise that your output path is audible.

Windows reports a missing VCRUNTIME or MSVCP file

Install Microsoft's current x64 Visual C++ Redistributable from Microsoft's official download page, then reopen the synth. Do not download individual DLLs from unrelated sites.

A missing cap, cracked key or broken bender looks alarming

These are visual AGE effects. The controls continue to work. Lower AGE to restore a fresher appearance; the same setting also returns the sound toward its healthy profile.

PROJECT NOTES

Made for curiosity & playing

KB-09 is an unofficial personal recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09, using a circuit-informed digital model and artwork modeled after his own aged instrument. “Authentic” names the original-style routing mode; individual hardware instruments can differ. This project is not affiliated with Roland Corporation and is not for sale.

The instrument has one voice, no onboard effects, no sequencer and no MIDI Learn. It reports 128 samples of processing latency. Hardware Gate/CV sockets, alternate tunings and per-note expressive protocols are not provided. Use your DAW for recording, effects and arrangement.
