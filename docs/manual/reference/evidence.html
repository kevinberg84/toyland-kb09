Formats and next steps

The present download is Windows x64 VST3 plus a standalone EXE. Finish owner testing of this build first. AU for Mac hosts and AAX Native for Pro Tools follow with native platform and host tests.

Apple describes Audio Unit compatibility in Logic Pro on Mac. AU is not a Windows format. Avid describes its AAX SDK and development/testing requirements. AAX SDK access, signing/deployment and Pro Tools testing must be resolved before offering an AAX download. No AU, AAX DSP or HDX build is included. Older VST formats are unnecessary for the current owner workflow.

The current HTML manual works offline and can be printed. A final paginated PDF, platform installers and public release licensing are later packaging tasks; this folder supports personal review.

DAW display evidence

Kevin confirmed that disabling Auto-Scale Plug-in Window solved sharpness in his Live setup. The report did not specify a precise Live build, monitor resolution/scaling, mixed-DPI test or fresh-instance action. The guide keeps those distinctions visible.

Other DAW recipes come from their individual vendor documentation and are linked in the guide. They are proposed native-rendering settings, not completed KB-09 host tests. Choose a DAW to see its instructions and source. Keep global HiDPI support enabled; disable compatibility bitmap enlargement only where needed.

Practical limits

KB-09 is a circuit-informed approximation. Its oscillator, filter, follower, drive and tapers have not been fitted to a hardware audio capture set. The supplied personal photographs improve visible geometry and wear; they do not measure alloys, chemistry or exact control travel.

The synth is monophonic and duplicates its mono output to stereo. External stereo input is averaged to mono, so antiphase signals can cancel. Fixed 4× oversampling reports 128 samples of latency. AGE is a repeatable condition/tolerance profile, not elapsed years. Modern MOD has deeper maxima than older builds; Authentic mappings remain unchanged.

The case photograph is 1774 × 887. A larger output does not add missing photographic detail; native labels and fine drawn details do render at output resolution. DAWs retain window authority. Physical 4K, mixed-monitor DPI, complete Live recall and every native gesture need their own checks.

Presets store parameters and the factory-program reference, including AGE. They do not store held notes, oscillator/filter phase, live envelope position, power or window placement.
