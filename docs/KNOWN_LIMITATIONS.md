# Known limitations and remaining fidelity work

Version **0.11.0** is a usable circuit-informed software instrument. No physical SH-09 capture set, validated full-circuit SPICE reference, blind listening test or completed hardware listening A/B is claimed. Public demonstrations and photographs are research references, not calibration data.

- The four nonlinear OTA cells and bounded feedback solver follow the four-BA662 schematic at a functional level. Normalised stages, saturation and diode feedback approximate the circuit rather than solve every transistor/op-amp. Self-oscillation level/tuning, stage interaction and drive require measurement.
- The follower preserves the pre-mixer tap and VCF route but uses positive rectification and three smoothing sections. Its active RC network, diode threshold, input sensitivity and volts-to-dBFS relationship are not exactly reproduced.
- Oscillator curvature, PWM edges, mixer/noise levels, VCA/output colour and control tapers have not been fitted to hardware. Published endpoints guide the maps. Bender depth maxima are engineering choices.
- **Modern compatibility:** 0.11 increases LFO MOD maxima to ±36 semitones VCO and ±8 octaves VCF. Authentic remains ±12/±5. Older Modern states with nonzero MOD can produce deeper modulation despite unchanged parameter values/IDs. These ranges are unmeasured and are separate from bender sensitivities.
- Age is a deterministic tolerance and appearance profile, not elapsed years or a measured historical unit. Gradual rust/patina/yellowing, individually appearing fixed-size/opacity stains, chipped branding, aged screw patches and two selected key cracks are illustrative. The clean key materials are shared, but all 32 key stain shapes are independent and used once; two independent cracks and three rotary corrosion marks are also assigned once. Crack onsets at 0.82/0.94 are artistic thresholds, not physical reliability predictions. All fader caps remain fitted. Default zero selects the healthy calibration profile.
- The bare main-panel fader actuator is not depicted: its cross-section and exposed height above the assembled panel remain unverified. Roland's [service notes, pp. 2–3](https://el34world.com/charts/Schematics/files/Roland/Roland_sh09_service_manual.pdf) distinguish main-panel LFE9R-C16 sliders from lower-bay EVA sliders. [Syntaur's used 029-306 listing](https://syntaur.com/Part-1602-Slide-potentiometer-100KA) specifies 30 mm travel and a 15 mm shaft; those figures do not establish the visible assembled projection.
- The GUI combines generated photographic assets and native controls. Smooth black paint, compact switches and a hidden-sector bender are reconstructions. The bender's inferred front-to-back axle/±35° projection uses cross-model PB-4 evidence; exact SH-09 travel and dimensions are unmeasured. The 1774 × 887 chassis cannot supply native 4K photographic detail when enlarged. A physical 4K display was unavailable.
- Actual editor-client pixel/chrome measurements improve M/L requests, but hosts retain window authority. Complete footer bounds are tested at wide/tall sizes. The preceding 0.11 standalone M/L were observed at 857 × 534 / 1280 × 720 logical units on a 1920 × 1080 physical display at 150% scaling, with the complete footer. Mixed-monitor DPI, native-caption top-snap, unsupported/docked hosts, actual native hover-popup placement and every mouse gesture remain unverified. See [window behaviour](GUI_WINDOW_BEHAVIOR.md).
- Fixed 4x oversampling reports 128 samples of latency, with no quality selector. MIDI is sample-offset aware; host parameter changes are smoothed from block updates. A single high stationary-saw alias regression is not a complete pitch/PWM/modulation grid.
- Stereo external input is averaged to mono; antiphase inputs cancel. Output is mono duplicated to stereo. This is a mono instrument path, not a stereo effects processor. External trim operates only in Modern.
- Sustain pedal, per-note expressive protocols, polyphony, unison, MIDI Learn, alternate tunings and effects are not implemented. Duplicate note-ons use reference counts.
- Host state and `.kb09`/compatible `.sh09state` files store parameters and factory-program reference. They do not store held notes, oscillator phase, filter state, current envelope position, power or window placement. Restoring a patch does not resume its waveform sample-for-sample. User files retain the built-in factory bank; they are not general `.vstpreset` or commercial patch importers.
- Power continues callbacks while muting output, consumes/discards MIDI and clears note/controller history on transitions. Fresh note-ons are required after power-up; external Hold audio can resume without them. This is not an analog power-cycle model.

Final October 7 results are **92 DSP checks, 20 processor checks, 58 preset-file checks, 280 GUI assertions, 47 Steinberg tests and 75 actual-VST3 host checks**, all without failures. GUI-enabled pluginval strictness 10 reports SUCCESS and confirmed exit 0. The expanded GUI fixture covers individual stain/crack events and distinct healthy/aged key samples. Processor allocation guards recorded zero tracked C++ allocations but cover only the tested operators/callbacks, not every allocator or host. The 12.68:1 value-bubble result checks configured colours, not complete accessibility. The 600-second actual-module offline benchmark is a single machine measurement, not a DAW CPU guarantee.

The final VST3 is installed with matching build/staging/module hashes recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md). Live-specific 0.11 testing was not repeated because desktop automation could not resolve Live's foreground process after three recovery attempts; [LIVE_TEST.md](LIVE_TEST.md) scopes earlier observations separately. Software passes do not establish sonic equivalence or freedom from defects in every host.
