# Known limitations and remaining fidelity work

For current 1.4.0 boundaries, see [the release validation](VALIDATION_1.4.0.md), [the audio investigation](ANALOG_INVESTIGATION_2026_10.md) and [the user manual](manual/index.html). The material below records **historical 0.13.2** behavior and formats; it is not the current release specification.

## Historical 0.13.2 record

Version **0.13.2** is a circuit-informed software instrument with control geometry and aging informed by Kevin's own multi-angle SH-09 photographs. This revision refines the v5 photographic knobs, gradual metal pitting/dulling, photo-referenced POWER area and immediate mouse-key feedback while retaining the shared 9° native projection target. Windows VST3, standalone and unsigned AAX Native development builds compile. Exact checks, package and installed-binary evidence belong in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md); compilation does not establish a new native DAW test. No physical SH-09 audio capture set, validated full-circuit SPICE reference, blind listening test or completed hardware listening A/B is claimed. Photographs inform the appearance rather than calibrate the sound.

- The four nonlinear OTA cells and bounded feedback solver follow the four-BA662 schematic at a functional level. Normalised stages, saturation and diode feedback approximate the circuit rather than solve every transistor/op-amp. Self-oscillation level/tuning, stage interaction and drive require measurement.
- Electronic age does not have a verified distortion curve. The SH-09's D12/D13 resonance-feedback clamp is present in a healthy instrument and already motivates the model's feedback limiter. AGE changes calibration and timing without changing the mixer/filter/VCA saturation scales. Additional distortion from deteriorated components is possible but unit- and fault-dependent; no automatic increase is claimed. See [age and distortion research](SH09_AGE_DISTORTION_RESEARCH.md). No extra age-driven saturation was introduced for 0.13.2; the underlying DSP remains unchanged from the preceding release.
- The follower preserves the pre-mixer tap and VCF route but uses positive rectification and three smoothing sections. Its active RC network, diode threshold, input sensitivity and volts-to-dBFS relationship are not exactly reproduced.
- Oscillator curvature, PWM edges, mixer/noise levels, VCA/output colour and control tapers have not been fitted to hardware. Published endpoints guide the maps. Bender depth maxima are engineering choices.
- **Modern compatibility:** 0.11 increases LFO MOD maxima to ±36 semitones VCO and ±8 octaves VCF. Authentic remains ±12/±5. Older Modern states with nonzero MOD can produce deeper modulation despite unchanged parameter values/IDs. These ranges are unmeasured and are separate from bender sensitivities.
- Age is a deterministic tolerance and appearance profile, not elapsed years or measured component deterioration. Dust, yellowing, dulling, knob micro-pitting and exposed-panel corrosion develop gradually. Independent pit patterns cover the silver tops and skirts of all three large knobs while reflections dull; the density and response are artistic estimates. Native pale white/grey deposits affect the registered case screws, with separately shaded and worn upper/lower POWER heads. The upper POWER screw is darker and tarnished; the lower develops broader pale oxidation. Their alloy and chemistry are not identified. Clean key materials remain shared, with independent per-key dust/grime and polish; fading bitmap highlights reveal opaque native matte surfaces, not transparent keys. The lower rail has localized fine matte grain. POWER has diffuse grey plastic, a broad rounded bevel and a tall OFF profile; its estimated physical height and travel are not measured hardware dimensions. The old wear atlas is no longer compiled or rendered. The fixed coffee ring appears at AGE 6 and is shifted +40 right/−45 up in design units to clear the envelope-source control.
- One photo-informed native hairline crack appears on MIDI note 60 at displayed AGE 5.6. Its 0.32-logical-unit stroke and 55% opacity stay fixed while its revealed length can grow from 78% to 100%. No second key crack is drawn. The VCO mixer cap is lost at 6.4, the RELEASE cap at 8.8, and the bender tip breaks at 9.2. These discrete thresholds are artistic condition choices, not hardware reliability predictions. Lowering AGE reverses them. The underlying controls keep working, and default zero selects the healthy calibration profile.
- The current v5 knob is a complete photographic sprite derived from the owner's selected object, retaining its physical barrel height, silver skirt and grouped ribs alternating with smooth panels. The silver tops and black rims were narrowed following the owner's overhead reference; the generated image does not prove measured geometry. Only the native black top tick rotates, with a length approximately one third of the silver top diameter; the hidden collar has no tick. The shared native target is 9° for the tick, rigid extruded bender and blade projections; 0.13's 18° native-cylinder renderer is historical. The approved overhead caps remain unchanged, with top-and-side stripes and black front lips; exposed blades show thin longitudinal edges with stepped shoulders. The volume control excludes the owner's replacement-knob geometry. Dimensions, projection and unbroken bender-tip geometry remain reconstructions, not measured part CAD or verified mechanical travel. Corrected ENV selector print places GATE / + / TRIG above the axis, GATE left and LFO below. The POWER legend uses photo-referenced filled ON/OFF profiles with restrained native print wear, and the lamp is below it. The bunny sticker and taped waveform marks are excluded.
- The GUI combines **nine** generated/user-supplied PNG inputs with native controls and wear detail. `knob-personal-overhead-v5.png` is the complete clean runtime sprite; its native moving tick, metal dulling and pitting are separate layers. Earlier v2/v3/v4 assets, the former `knob-personal.png` material mapping and rejected flattened candidates are historical. Finite-resolution textures cannot gain detail merely by enlarging a 3840 × 2160 editor export. Native lettering, crack, dust, tick, POWER surfaces and blade geometry render at the requested output scale. Physical 4K-display behavior still needs its own observation. [Artwork inventory](../Assets/Photoreal/README.md) and [provenance](../Assets/Photoreal/PROMPTS.md) distinguish runtime inputs from historical research assets.
- Actual editor-client pixel/chrome measurements improve S/M/L requests, but hosts retain window authority. S uses a 65% work-area width/height bound, M 90%, and L the work area. Complete footer/header bounds and restoration are exercised by the compiled GUI fixture. Mixed-monitor DPI, native-caption top-snap, unsupported/docked hosts, actual native hover-popup placement and every mouse gesture are separate host-specific checks. Kevin confirmed that disabling Live's Auto-Scale Plug-in Window fixed his display; this does not establish all other DAW scaling recipes. See [window behaviour](GUI_WINDOW_BEHAVIOR.md) and the [manual's DAW sections](manual/index.html#daws).
- Mouse presses, drags and release now update the effective-note amber marker immediately; MIDI and mouse notes retain lowest-note priority, and other held notes are blue. The marker is drawn at the held key's lower edge and disappears after release. This fixes the earlier mouse-feedback delay without establishing every host's native mouse-capture behavior.
- Fixed 4x oversampling reports 128 samples of latency, with no quality selector. MIDI is sample-offset aware; host parameter changes are smoothed from block updates. A single high stationary-saw alias regression is not a complete pitch/PWM/modulation grid.
- Stereo external input is averaged to mono; antiphase inputs cancel. Output is mono duplicated to stereo. This is a mono instrument path, not a stereo effects processor. External trim operates only in Modern.
- Sustain pedal, per-note expressive protocols, polyphony, unison, MIDI Learn, alternate tunings and effects are not implemented. Duplicate note-ons use reference counts.
- Host state and `.kb09`/compatible `.sh09state` files store parameters and factory-program reference. They do not store held notes, oscillator phase, filter state, current envelope position, power or window placement. Restoring a patch does not resume its waveform sample-for-sample. User files retain the built-in factory bank; they are not general `.vstpreset` or commercial patch importers.
- Power continues callbacks while muting output, consumes/discards MIDI and clears note/controller history on transitions. Fresh note-ons are required after power-up; external Hold audio can resume without them. This is not an analog power-cycle model.
- Windows VST3, standalone and AAX Native compile from the shared 0.13.2 source. The AAX bundle is **unsigned development material and untested in Pro Tools**; ordinary Pro Tools loading requires the separate AAX signing route. No signing credentials or Pro Tools developer host are configured here, so the bundle must be clearly separated from the ready-to-use VST3 installation flow. AU is configured for native macOS but **unbuilt and untested** because no Mac/Xcode host is available; a Mac source kit is not an installable AU. Mac signing/notarization, AU `auval` and native host tests remain outstanding. [Format status](FORMAT_ROADMAP.md) and [build instructions](BUILD.md) record the exact boundaries. The updated offline [HTML manual](manual/index.html) starts with instrument features and covers playing, AGE, presets, installation and DAW settings. The consumer guide omits the separate technical evidence page. Vendor-guided recipes remain distinct from actual KB-09 host observations.

Release-specific check counts, process exits, logs and module hashes are maintained in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). Historical 0.11–0.13.1 passes apply to those binaries, not the changed 0.13.2 rendering or AAX wrapper. Processor allocation guards cover the tested operators/callbacks rather than every allocator or host. Value-bubble contrast assertions check configured colours, not complete accessibility. Actual-module offline benchmarks are bounded single-machine measurements, not DAW CPU guarantees.

Installed/staged identity and acceptance are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md). [LIVE_TEST.md](LIVE_TEST.md) keeps earlier native observations separate from the owner's Auto-Scale confirmation. Software passes do not establish sonic equivalence or freedom from defects in every host.
