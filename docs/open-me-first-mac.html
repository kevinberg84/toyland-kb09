Let's play KB-09.

A recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09, with a few cool extras. Mac Intel and Apple Silicon · Version 0.13.21 · UNVERIFIED.

Download the Mac ZIP from the shared Drive folder. Double-click Toyland KB-09 for Mac - UNVERIFIED.zip to extract it. Open the extracted folder and keep it together. The original ZIP preserves Mac executable permissions.

Wanna play immediately? Try the standalone first!

Double-click Toyland KB-09 Standalone, the app beside this page. No plug-in installation needed.

Click AUDIO / MIDI in its top bar to choose your speakers or headphones and enable your controller under Active MIDI inputs. Or enable KEYBOARD to play with your computer keyboard.

More Detailed Standalone Instructions

Ready to use it in your music software?

Close your music software.

  1. Double-click INSTALL KB-09.command beside this page. Press Return when prompted and enter your Mac login password; password typing stays hidden. The installer puts the AU and VST3 in the correct places.
  2. Reopen your music software. In Logic Pro or GarageBand, choose Toyland KB-09 as an Audio Unit instrument. Other compatible hosts can use VST3. Rescan plug-ins if needed.
Developer cannot be verified? This test build is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.
