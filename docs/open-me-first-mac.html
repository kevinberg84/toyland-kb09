Let's play KB-09.

A recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09, with a few cool extras. Mac Intel and Apple Silicon · Version 1.8.3.

Download the Mac ZIP from the shared Drive folder. Double-click Toyland KB-09 for Mac.zip to extract it. Open the extracted folder and keep it together. The original ZIP preserves Mac executable permissions.

Wanna play immediately? Try the standalone first!

Double-click Toyland KB-09 Standalone, the app beside this page. No plug-in installation needed.

Click AUDIO / MIDI in its top bar to choose your speakers or headphones and enable your controller under Active MIDI inputs. Or enable KEYBOARD to play with your computer keyboard.

More Detailed Standalone Instructions

Want an installed app and desktop shortcut?

Run INSTALL KB-09.command. It installs the standalone in your home Applications folder and offers an optional desktop shortcut. Choose No for AU/VST3 if you do not use a DAW. Saved presets and audio/MIDI preferences are kept.

Ready to use it in your music software?

Keep the plug-in sharp: use KB-09’s S/M/L buttons to resize its panel. If the AU or VST3 looks blurry, check your host for a plug-in scaling or compatibility option; if offered, let KB-09 handle its own scale. The name and availability depend on the host and format. See display troubleshooting and your DAW’s instructions.

Close your music software.

  1. Double-click INSTALL KB-09.command beside this page. Choose Yes when asked to install AU/VST3 plug-ins. Enter your Mac login password when prompted; password typing stays hidden.
  2. Reopen your music software. In Logic Pro or GarageBand, choose Toyland KB-09 as an Audio Unit instrument. Other compatible hosts can use VST3. Rescan plug-ins if needed.

Skipped the desktop shortcut?

Rerun INSTALL KB-09.command and answer Yes to the standalone desktop shortcut prompt. On Mac, decline AU/VST3 if you only want the standalone shortcut. Your saved presets and preferences are retained. Opening the portable standalone itself does not offer that prompt.
