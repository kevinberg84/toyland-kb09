## JUCE dependency

JUCE modules offer AGPLv3 and the alternative JUCE EULA. A combined application cannot
be distributed under the AGPL option with an additional no-sale restriction. Kevin
confirmed personal ownership and the JUCE Starter financial eligibility criterion.
Eligibility is not a substitute for following the applicable EULA and dependency notices.

The interaction between JUCE EULA section 1.11 and the first-party no-sale restriction
has not been independently resolved. Section 1.4 recognizes the owner's rights in their
contribution; section 1.11 addresses recipients' distribution and sale rights. We have
not established that the custom restriction violates the EULA, nor obtained a binding
interpretation that confirms its compatibility. No JUCE correspondence was sent and
no compatibility or enforceability guarantee is asserted.
