# GUI proposal — revision 3, 12 October 2026

Approved by Kevin on 12 October 2026 for release 1.5.0, with the additional request for lighter white-key scuffs at Sold As-Is. The final scuff ramp starts after Vintage, Baby! and retains the approved Fire Hazard endpoint. Release validation is recorded separately.

The performance bay paint extends to X327, behind the first key at X326, and slightly left/up to X60/Y364. All controls, interior outlines, keyboard geometry, and the textured strip retain their revision-2 positions. The outer bay outline is open at the top. The margin between the right printed rule and first key is now part of the painted bay.

The five appearance names are Minty, Seen Some Shit, Vintage, Baby!, Sold As-Is, and Fire Hazard. Sold As-Is replaces Smells Like 1983. Manual wording and screenshots will follow these names when the GUI is approved.

Cheek scrapes receive a fixed birth stage and retain their final contrast after appearing. Successive stages add local marks. Low-contrast plastic dulling remains gradual across stages; the approved modest wear and darker right cheek remain.

Only Fire Hazard gets stronger white-key micro-scuffs: 55 instead of 45 fine traces per key, 1.65 times the previous trace opacity, modestly longer/wider paths, and distinct per-key placement. Earlier white-key stages, the approved black keys, cap rendering, and footer scales remain unchanged.

The gallery includes actual compiled editor renders at five stages, a previous/revised maximum-age white-key comparison, and detail crops. Regression results and source hashes are in its preview manifest. No DSP or processor source changed.
