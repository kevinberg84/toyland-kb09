# POWER cap and panel details — 7 October 2026

**Release follow-up:** the cap, label and differentiated screw-wear work is included in installed 0.13.2. Earlier preview-only statements below retain their historical scope; see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) for the release results.

The owner clarified that the POWER cap **should stand tall in OFF**. The earlier reduction to a shallow OFF cap was a misinterpretation. This correction retains the shared near-overhead camera and grey material, and raises the same cap in OFF without stretching its face or changing its viewing angle.

## Evidence

- Roland's [SH-09 Owner's Manual, printed page 7](https://thismodernday.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Roland-SH-09-Owners-Manual.pdf#page=8) identifies a push-on/push-off power switch with a separate LED indicator. The existing local page render was visually inspected.
- Roland's [27 March 1980 Service Notes](https://el34world.com/charts/Schematics/files/Roland/Roland_sh09_service_manual.pdf) identify grey Button no. 8, part 016-008, in the first-page product photograph and third-page parts list. The existing local first-page render was visually inspected. Neither manual supplies a cap-height or latch-travel dimension.
- The owner's `20261007_131020.jpg` shows the broad flat top, softened edges and substantial vertical body in an oblique view of the performance bay. `20261007_131236.jpg` and `20261007_131314.jpg` provide overhead context. Photo colour is not calibrated, and the photographs alone do not establish switch state or travel. The owner's explicit clarification establishes the raised OFF state requested here.
- The owner's later [POWER close-up](../Research/SH09_power_owner_closeup.png), preserved unchanged from the supplied clipboard image, resolves a broad rounded bevel around a flat central face. The front bevel carries a soft highlight; the cap is diffuse grey plastic rather than a white key with a sharp outline. Its conspicuous right side reflects the photograph's oblique lateral view, which is not copied into the plugin's shared near-overhead camera.
- The same close-up places a screw above the cap and another below it, followed by the POWER legend and then the lamp. The legend has two solid side-profile button diagrams, depressed above and raised below, with ON/OFF to their right. The upper screw is dark and tarnished with scattered pale deposits; the lower head is much more broadly chalky grey/white, with irregular dark patches and a deep worn Phillips recess. The colour is not calibrated and the chemical composition of these deposits is not established by the photograph.

## Rendering

The top has identical dimensions in both states. Estimated physical depths of 18 design units OFF and 7 ON project through the common 9° camera to approximately 2.82 and 1.10 design units of visible front wall. The top moves approximately 1.72 design units between states. These are visual reconstruction estimates, not measured hardware dimensions.

The subsequent close-up correction replaces the thin inset outline and hard top/body seam with a 1.8-unit rounded bevel around a flat centre, with a 2-unit outer corner radius. A cached native CPU surface at six pixels per design unit shades continuous bevel normals, diffuse grey plastic, a soft front-corner reflection and stable fine moulding grain. The raised body remains darker underneath, with a height-dependent contact shadow. Camera, cap footprint and button hit target are retained.

The upper POWER screw is relocated from the earlier photographic corner to the cap axis; nearby registered panel material covers that earlier head before screen printing. A second screw sits below the cap. The two small domed heads use independently seeded native CPU material fields: curved dome shading, connected oxidation at several scales, fine gritty texture, and an irregular tapered Phillips socket with shaded inner walls. The upper head darkens with tarnish while the lower develops broad pale oxidation. Wear progresses continuously and reversibly with AGE. The legend is redrawn with filled button profiles and restrained fixed alpha-mask print loss, so small worn areas reveal the actual panel. The lamp moves below it, leaving clearance above PORTAMENTO. Native construction is in `Source/GUI/PowerDetails.h` and `Source/GUI/Editor.cpp`.

The earlier height-only exports remain in `artifacts/GUI-power-tall-off-preview/`. The close-up iteration exports a wider crop including both screws, the label and lamp in `artifacts/GUI-power-closeup-preview/`: `power_age0_off.png`, `power_age0_on.png`, `power_age7_off.png` and `power_age7_on.png`, plus full performance-bay and panel images. The final GUI-only build succeeds, and `GUIRender.log` reports **347 checks, zero failures**, including real POWER callbacks in both states. All four final detail exports and the aged performance bay were visually inspected: raised/lowered cap, diffuse grey bevel, dark upper versus pale lower screw oxidation, subtle print wear, filled profile symbols, lamp placement and clearance. The Small layout was also inspected. Automated checks establish preview behaviour, not measured hardware fidelity or owner aesthetic acceptance.

Only `SH09GUIRender` was built. The installed 0.13.1 VST3, standalone and package remain unchanged.
