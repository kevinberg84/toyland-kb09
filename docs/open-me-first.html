A security warning will likely appear.

Your computer may initially block KB-09 or its installer. Follow the instructions for your system below.

Mac: “Apple could not verify” / “Not Opened”

If you received this copy from Kevin and trust it, choose Done rather than Move to Trash. After the blocked opening attempt, go to Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security. Find the KB-09 item and choose Open Anyway, authenticate if asked, and confirm Open. Try the standalone or installer again. Repeat for a separately blocked installer if needed. If Open Anyway is unavailable, send Kevin the exact message. Apple’s illustrated instructions.

Windows: “Windows protected your PC”

If you received this copy from Kevin and trust it, click More info, then Run anyway. A separate installation permission prompt may ask you to choose Yes. If Run anyway is unavailable, contact Kevin or your computer administrator.

This is Kevin’s free personal project. It does not have verified developer signing: the Mac release is ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization; the Windows release is unsigned. That is why these warnings can appear. Leave your system’s security enabled. Do not override a warning that specifically says malware was found or the software will damage your computer.

Let's play KB-09.

A recreation of Kevin's Roland SH-09, with a few cool extras. Windows 64-bit · Version 1.6.0.

Received a ZIP file? Right-click it, choose Extract All..., then Extract. Open the newly extracted folder first.

Wanna play immediately? Try the standalone first!

Double-click Toyland KB-09 Standalone, the Application with the keyboard icon beside this page. No installation needed. Windows usually hides its .exe extension.

Click AUDIO / MIDI in its top bar to choose your speakers or headphones and enable your controller under Active MIDI inputs. Or turn on KEYBOARD to play with your computer keyboard.

More Detailed Standalone Instructions

Ready to use it in your music software?

Close your music software.

  1. Go back to the folder beside this page. Double-click INSTALL KB-09 (the file ending in .cmd). If Windows asks whether to allow changes, choose Yes. In the installation window, press Enter when prompted.
    The installer puts everything in the correct place. You do not need to copy or choose any files.
  2. Open your music software. Find Toyland KB-09 in its instrument list and add it to an instrument/MIDI track. If it is missing, rescan VST3 plug-ins in your music software's settings, then reopen the software.
