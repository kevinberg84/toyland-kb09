## Bender evidence and limits

The user's broken unit establishes the **wheel construction** especially clearly. The broad arched rim and front channels remain visible even with the finger tip missing. A faithful intact rendering therefore needs a wheel/sector body in the slot and a raised tip attached to that body's crown. A flat horizontal fader, an isolated joystick, or a full circular dial seen from above does not match these observations.

The intact raised tip was checked against the upper original unit in the repairer's [two-unit photograph](https://snw.lonningdal.no/sh09/Roland_SH-09_repair_01.jpg), displayed locally as `Research/SH09_front_reference.jpg`. The [repair page](https://snw.lonningdal.no/sh09.php) establishes the source. The same image provides a second unit at a different angle, but its tip is not clearly intact. The owner's [near-top photograph](https://www.cykong.com/Synths/Roland%20SH-09/Images/Orig%20SH-09%20Top.jpg), displayed locally as `Research/SH09_Kong_top_reference.jpg`, corroborates the opening and overall location; its small tip is not sufficiently resolved for a precise profile.

Roland's [SH09 Service Notes](https://synthfool.com/docs/Roland/SH_Series/Roland%20SH09%20Service%20Manual.pdf), parts list p. 3, identify the PB-4 assembly. The [owner's manual](https://thismodernday.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Roland-SH-09-Owners-Manual.pdf) describes opposing left/right bend around the centre. The firsthand [Synth.nl repair account, June 14, 2009](https://synthnl.blogspot.com/2009/06/) describes replacing the plastic bender on a potentiometer, with return springs on its sides; this supports the hidden axle and spring return, not the chosen projection dimensions.

New searches located [Studio Repair's SH-09 gallery](https://www.studiorepair.com/gallery/Roland/SH-09/index.html) and [controller detail](https://www.studiorepair.com/gallery/Roland/SH-09/slides/Roland_SH-09_LHC__STUDIOREPAIR_07012701_0702185493.html), but page requests timed out. These images were **not** inspected and are not used as geometry evidence. An official [Roland 1978 catalogue](https://www.roland.com/jp/lp/catalog_museum/pdf/NoNAM-0021.pdf) was located, but the tool did not expose a usable visual screenshot; its text is not a substitute for a viewed close-up.

Consequently, the curved body is strongly photo-supported; the exact intact tip outline, axle depth, viewing projection and angular throw remain estimates. There is no claim that an intact tip was measured from multiple high-resolution views.

## Native rendering changes

`Source/GUI/Editor.cpp` now uses native paths for the following details:

- **Small unindexed switch grips:** a raised rounded tongue, dark front and side, six transverse ribs and restrained grey edge highlights. The utility sliders' indexed caps retain their existing rendering. No additional image or repeated dirt stamp is used.
- **Bender wheel:** a broad arched rim with a shallow front channel, a small solid paddle, and a hidden axle projection. The opening and component bounds remain unchanged. The drawing uses the existing approximate ±35° visual throw; this is an engineering value, not a measured original stop angle.
- **Broken bender event:** at normalized Age `0.92` (displayed 9.2), the full paddle is replaced discretely by a short irregular stump. The wheel remains. This is cosmetic: the existing parameter mapping, bend amount, spring return and audio operation remain active.
- **Envelope source legend:** restored the separate plus sign between GATE and TRIG, using three native printed rows. This supersedes the earlier low-resolution inference in `SH09_CONTROL_GEOMETRY_RESEARCH.md`.

The implementation deliberately uses dark plastic and modest grey highlights for the switch/bender. Red oxide is not inferred on those molded surfaces. It does not copy the replacement Volume knob or infer the fader blade's metal alloy from colour.

Screw heads should develop localized pale grey/white oxidation-like deposits and dull highlights as requested. The nearby panel can retain brown corrosion. The photographs do not establish a fastener alloy or chemical identification, and the pale deposit should not be described as proof of aluminium. Avoid a uniform white disk: retain the cross/slot recess, dark valleys and some exposed dull metal.

For aged keys, lower and soften narrow baked specular highlights while retaining ivory midtones, side shading and key separation. A useful initial art-direction target is a 30–50% reduction in the strongest edge-highlight contrast at high Age, with broader softer reflections. Whole-key darkening or stronger yellow alone does not remove gloss. The photographs still contain glints on aged keys; the more satin endpoint is the user's requested appearance, not a claim that every aged original has the same roughness.
