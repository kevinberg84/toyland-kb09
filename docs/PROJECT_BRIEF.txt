PROJECT: BUILD A HIGH-FIDELITY ROLAND SH-09 VST3 EMULATION

You are the primary engineer for this project.

Your job is to research, design, implement, compile, validate, debug, correct,
and deliver a working Windows x64 VST3 emulation of the Roland SH-09 for use
primarily in Ableton Live.

This is NOT a request for:

- sample code
- a mockup
- an unfinished prototype
- a proof of concept
- a design document without a working product

The required deliverable is a genuinely usable compiled VST3 instrument.

You are expected to work autonomously through research, implementation,
testing, debugging, correction, rebuilding, and retesting before presenting
the result.

The project should be treated as a serious attempt at recreating the original
SH-09 rather than merely producing an SH-09-inspired subtractive synthesizer.

======================================================================
CORE PRIORITIES
======================================================================

Priorities, in order:

1. Sonic fidelity to the original SH-09.
2. Behavioral fidelity to the original SH-09.
3. Accurate recreation of the external audio input and envelope follower.
4. Stability and real-time audio safety.
5. Excellent integration with Ableton Live.
6. Photorealistic reproduction of the physical SH-09.
7. Useful optional modern extensions that do not alter Authentic mode.
8. Efficient CPU usage consistent with the required sound quality.

Do not sacrifice clearly audible fidelity merely to reduce CPU usage.

Do not claim circuit accuracy, hardware accuracy, or measured equivalence
unless evidence actually supports that claim.
