## Final prompt

Create a production sprite sheet of PHOTOREALISTIC steel Phillips screw heads for a vintage analog synthesizer GUI. Exactly SIX COLUMNS by FIVE ROWS, 30 isolated heads in a strictly regular grid, transparent background. No text, no labels, no panel, no checkerboard printed into image. Each head centered at its cell center, identical diameter, generous uniform margin. Camera nearly perpendicular to heads, just a tiny view of their lower edge. These are low shallow pan-head machine screws, NOT hemispherical domes, NOT countersunk conical heads. The head has a broad nearly flat subtly convex crown, narrow rolled rim, real machined steel microtexture. True dark Phillips driver cavity, precise intersecting tapered driver slots with narrow metal walls; NO broad dished bowl surrounding the driver socket, NO grey filling inside the socket. No cartoony plus marks. A photographer's soft upper-left light gives restrained realistic metallic reflections and narrow darker lower-right rim, contact shadow subtle and compact. SAME light direction, head size, material geometry, and driver orientation in each column through all rows. Slight distinct original Phillips orientations between columns. ROW 1: new uncorroded silvery steel, restrained natural sheen, fine machining texture. ROW 2: slight handling wear, minute brown oxidation specks. ROW 3: older, duller, mottled fine scattered surface oxidation, real steel remains visible. ROW 4: heavily worn with natural rusty mottling, exactly column 3 and column 5 have visibly stripped rounded-out irregular torn driver sockets, others retain Phillips recesses. ROW 5: very old rusty worn steel, photographic finely granular reddish brown and dark oxidation with surviving metal, no bright glints; same column 3 and 5 stripped heads, other four intact. Rust is fine irregular mottled and pitted photographic corrosion, NOT large uniform brown polygons or painted patches. Shallow crown remains recognizable in all rows, dark contact shadows and socket depth retained. Think high quality macro product photography of actual screws, not vector, not illustration, not stylized 3D, not game icons. The complete 6x5 array must fit inside the canvas, all 30 heads separated, all unobscured, no extra objects. Aim wide enough per head for fine photographic detail.
